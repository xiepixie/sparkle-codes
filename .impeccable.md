## Design Context

### Users
This project is a personal technical blog and documentation platform. The primary audience is readers who come here to explore the author's writing, notes, and technical documentation. Core journeys include landing on the homepage to understand the author's taste and technical point of view, browsing blog listings to discover content, reading long-form technical posts without distraction, and navigating documentation with high clarity and strong information hierarchy.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel professional, refined, and UX-driven. It should express the identity of `sparkle.codes`: visibly luminous, memorable, and crafted, while still feeling credible and technically rigorous. The homepage in particular should communicate both aesthetic taste and engineering ability. The emotional tone should balance confidence and polish with a sense of glow, depth, and quiet delight rather than loud marketing energy.

### Aesthetic Direction
The current and intended direction is a purple starry-space visual system with an emphasis on dark mode. Dark surfaces, subtle glow, layered atmospherics, and premium interaction details should be used to create a distinctive "sparkle" identity. Supabase is a useful interaction reference, especially for elegant motion, premium depth, and polished hover states. The interface should not feel template-driven, generic SaaS, or over-decorated. Color choices should stay within the established purple cosmic theme and avoid combinations that weaken harmony, legibility, or the project's premium tone. Accessibility should meet at least WCAG AA.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with technical credibility, then add personality through restraint.
2. Make the "sparkle" identity feel atmospheric and premium, not flashy or gimmicky.
3. Prioritize dark-mode composition, contrast, and glow hierarchy across all major pages.
4. Treat UX polish as part of the brand: navigation, reading flow, hover states, and spacing should all feel intentional.
5. Avoid template aesthetics by giving each core page a clear role and visual rhythm while preserving a unified system.
6. Maintain at least WCAG AA contrast and interaction clarity, especially within purple-on-dark combinations.
